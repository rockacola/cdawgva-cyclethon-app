# 2026-09-12

## What I worked on

Ran `npm audit` and resolved all 15 flagged vulnerabilities (13 via `npm audit fix`, the last 2 via a forced Next.js 15 → 16.3.5 major bump, at the operator's direction). Also added `.ai/standards/writing-style.md`, adapted from the equivalent standard in `travislin.com_v5` for this project's product-copy context.

## Decisions made

- **Forced Next.js 16 upgrade accepted.** The remaining postcss vulnerability only resolved through a major Next bump; operator chose to attempt it rather than leave it.
- **`next lint` migrated to plain `eslint`.** Next 16 removed the `next lint` subcommand. Ran the official `@next/codemod next-lint-to-eslint-cli`, then scoped `lint`/`lint:fix` to `eslint src` (rather than the codemod's default `eslint .`) to preserve the same file coverage `next lint` had (pages/app/components/lib/src), avoiding scope creep into `scripts/` and `cypress/`.
- **Restored `@eslint/eslintrc` devDependency.** The codemod dropped it from `package.json` even though `eslint.config.mjs` still imports `FlatCompat` from it for the `prettier` config extension — would have broken a clean install.
- **Bumped `eslint-config-next` to 16.3.5** to match the new `next` version.
- **Fixed 4 newly-surfaced `react-hooks` lint errors** (purity, set-state-in-effect, use-memo) that the upgraded `eslint-config-next` now catches. Used the existing `useNow()` hook in place of raw `Date.now()` calls in the two donation feed components, and the render-time "adjust state when a prop changes" pattern in `Header.tsx` instead of an effect.
- **Writing style guide adapted, not copied.** Source guide is written for a first-person personal blog (Travis speaking directly, MDX notes). This app has no first-person narrator and no blog, so voice/tone was rewritten for product/donation-tracker copy; the Notes/Blog section was dropped entirely. Language rules (no em dashes, numerals, Australian/UK English, banned-patterns list) carried over largely as-is — UK spelling already matches existing usage in `docs/` ("colour mode").

## What I learned

- Next.js 16 auto-rewrites `tsconfig.json` on build/typecheck (`jsx: preserve` → `jsx: react-jsx`, adds `.next/dev/types/**/*.ts` to `include`). Expected, not a bug; just needed reformatting to match Prettier style.
- A scan of `src/messages/en.json` against the new writing-style standard turns up a couple of banned-pattern hits worth a future cleanup pass ("incredible community coming together", "empower families") — not fixed in this session since it's a separate content-editing task from tooling/security work.

## Problems / friction

`npm audit fix --force` and its codemod each made assumptions (widened lint scope, dropped a still-needed dependency) that needed manual correction rather than trusting the automated output as final.
